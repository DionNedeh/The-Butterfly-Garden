# 4.0: packaging the first Android app

Written on 2 November 2026, when the final run ended with 3.9.0 live. On that day the owner split what the run left into two plans:

- **This plan** holds the work toward 4.0, the first release of the Android app on Google Play. That is everything about packaging and releasing the app, and the remaining part of each item the run left partial.
- **[The future update plan](future-update.md)** holds every decision that waits on the owner, and the rest of the future work.

Where an item here depends on a choice, it says so ("waits on D-n") and the decision itself is in the future update plan. Nothing here is built yet: this is planning only.

The full text of each numbered item is its step in [the final-PR plan](../final-pr-plan.md). What the final run did on each is in [its execution plan](../final-run/execution-plan.md). The Android decisions and phases are in [android-play-plan.md](../android-play-plan.md), and the store material and checklists are in [play-store/README.md](../play-store/README.md).

## Where things stand

- **The app.** 3.9.0 is live on the web. The Android app is the same React app bundled by Capacitor 8.5.3; [android-play-plan.md](../android-play-plan.md) holds its decisions.
  - Phases 0 to 4 are built.
  - Phase 5, the release candidate, is under way. `android.yml`'s `release` job makes the signed bundle, on request, from the `play-release` environment's secrets.
- **It is not on Google Play yet.**
  - The Pass licence key in `android/app/src/main/res/values/garden_pass.xml` is empty, so the Pass is never granted.
  - The owner's Play Console steps have not started.
- **First-load budget.** Android's first load is 169,025 of 169,245 bytes: 220 bytes spare. Anything 4.0 adds to the first load must be offset or lazy. The budget's numbers are decision D-10.
- **Checks paused while the app is being built.** Since 1 November, pull requests run every check except the Safari and Firefox job. A push to `main` re-runs only the quick checks before deploying. Both come back before 4.0 is submitted (P-17).

  - Section 2's release-level visual, accessibility and offline acceptance, from [section-2-follow-ups.md](../section-2-follow-ups.md).
- **P-22: the closed test and the Pass (7.1's done-when).**
  - A signed bundle from `android.yml`'s release job is in internal testing.
  - A licence tester can subscribe, cancel and see the account-hold path.

  - The closed test: at least 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days, then the application for production access and the production release (release step 8).

## Suggested order

1. **The owner's steps P-1 and P-2**, in parallel with the engineering that waits on nothing: P-5 (all but a night screenshot) and P-16 (a).
2. **The decisions that gate 4.0:** D-1 to D-6 (the Pass's price and trial, the PWA preview, the audience, which Android items go in, the lock's time limit, and the policy wording), and D-12 (a night install screenshot) before P-5 is finished.
3. **The chosen items:** P-6 to P-10, and the lock's time limit (P-13).
4. **Play Console (P-3 and P-4):** the first bundle, the app, its declarations and Data safety form, internal testing, the licence key, the subscription at D-1's prices, and the licence testers.
5. **Bring the paused checks back (P-17)** and get three green Safari and Firefox runs (P-16 b).
6. **The device checks** (P-14, P-16 e, P-20, P-21), then **4.0 itself** (P-12), the declarations checked again against its bundle (P-4), and the closed test through to production (P-22). The closed test takes at least 14 days.

Not needed for 4.0, unless the owner says otherwise: P-11 (it waits on Capacitor), P-15 (the move), P-16 (c) and (d), P-18 and P-19. Each follows its decision.
